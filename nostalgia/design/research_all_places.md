# NOSTALGIA: All important places (round 11b completeness sweep)

Purpose: the round 11 user request was "Birla Planetarium and Nehru Zoo park and all other
important places". `birla_planetarium` and `nehru_zoo` are already in `places_registry.md`
(round 11). This file is the completeness sweep: every other important place of early-2000s
Hyderabad and Secunderabad that is **not already in the registry**, with what the artist,
the map maker and the story team need.

Read with `places_registry.md` (binding ids and rules), `research_places.md` (geography,
anachronism blacklist, legends) and `research_mustplaces.md` (detail on the round 1 to 10
must-haves). This file does not repeat them.

## How this list was made

- Two researchers proposed 86 places. This pass removed duplicates (against each other and
  against every id in `places_registry.md`), removed anything not open or visible in 2000-2003,
  removed weak entries a kid would not remember, and re-checked every entry the researchers
  had marked unverified with a web search (Oct 2026). Entries that could not be verified were
  dropped (see "Dropped" at the end).
- **Result: 74 places survive, 12 were dropped** (1 duplicate, 9 unverifiable or unverified
  for the era, 2 weak).
- Four survivors are **sub-locations** inside an existing registry place. They get their own
  id because they are a separate building or shop a kid names on its own:
  `jawahar_bal_bhavan` (in `public_gardens`), `famous_ice_cream` (in `mozamjahi_market`),
  `hotel_madina` (in the Madina building of `pathergatti`), `golconda_jagadamba_temple` (in
  `golconda_fort`).
- `st_josephs_cathedral` gives an id to the church the registry already names as a
  `church_*` example. `khairatabad_mosque` and `jama_masjid_charminar` are named
  `mosque_*` instances. `garden_cafe`, `blue_sea_cafe`, `grand_hotel_abids`, `niloufer_cafe`,
  `hotel_madina` and `sarvi` are named landmark cafes on top of the generic `irani_cafe_*`
  instances.

## Tags and hard rules

- A plain statement was proposed as verified by the round 11b researchers or checked in this
  pass. **(unverified)** = plausible but not confirmed: fine for flavour, never for a trivia
  card. **(single source)** = one source only: fine for flavour, avoid on trivia cards.
  **(legend)** / **(anecdote)** = folklore or a story told about the place: never present as fact.
- **Era:** the story is set in 2000-2003. Anything after the chosen year is an anachronism (see
  "Anachronism notes from this sweep" below and `research_places.md` section 3).
- **Respect:** temples, mosques, dargahs, churches, the gurudwara and the ashurkhana are
  walk-only zones: no chases, no mischief, no Chappal Meter gain, footwear left at the gate.
  Bonalu scenes show no alcohol or animal-offering imagery. Hospitals show no scary imagery.
  Murgi Chowk shows cages and the queue, never slaughter.
- **People:** do not name shop founders or owners in game text (the rule in
  `research_mustplaces.md`); trivia cards say "the owner", "one of its Iranian founders" and so
  on. Do not name living politicians or film stars. Historical rulers and nobles (Nizams, Qutb
  Shahis, Paigahs) may be named.
- **Signboards:** these landmark names may appear on their own buildings. The generic
  `irani_cafe_*` and shop instances must not reuse them (rule in `research_mustplaces.md`).
  The Begumpet department store uses an invented name on its sign.
- Several of these places, or places near them, later saw tragic events. Never reference or
  hint at them.

**Game-use key:** story = story beat or chapter scene; side = side activity or minigame;
shop = shop or refill; clue = clue or view-point; coll = collectible; amb = ambience;
trivia = trivia (trump) card; travel = bus hub or fast-travel node.
