# NOSTALGIA, Pitch A: "SIDE B" (Heart)

*Gurthunda?* (Do you remember?)

**Logline.** In 2026, Srikanth, 34, a HITEC City manager, gets a courier from New Jersey: a worn cassette and a sketch-pen map from Immu, the best friend who left for America in 2003. PLAY rewinds him to Sankranti 2003: he is eleven, and Immu's family flies out the night after Eid. The boys swear to record the whole city's sound on one tape, keep a token from every place in a biscuit tin, and hide it on Chand Raat at Charminar, where a legendary tunnel begins.

**Era.** Tue 14 Jan to Tue 25 Nov 2003 (Chand Raat). Chinnu, born 1992, has 90s toys, TV and slang. Rythu Bazaars and NTR Gardens are open; Prasads IMAX opens *during* the story (Fri 25 Jul, mission 9); Begumpet is the airport; no mobiles, metro or malls. 2026 is only the prologue and epilogue.

## Cast

- **Chinnu (K. Srikanth)**, 11 (34 in 2026). Hawai chappals, scabbed knees, Walkman on a shoelace; quick Telugu-Dakhni, *pakka* about everything. *Wants* to stop the year; learns to give Immu the city instead.
- **Immu (Mohammed Imran)**, 11 (34). Taped glasses, CID notebook, Hero Ranger cycle. Records spy-message clues: "Agent Immu to Agent Chinnu, over." *Wants* not to go; failing that, to take Hyderabad along.
- **Amma, "Mummy" (Padma)**, 38. Jasmine in her bun, the legendary chappal. Telugu-first; ends on your full name. *Wants* you home before the street lights. Chappal Meter level 5.
- **Ammamma (Kamala)**, 68. Glasses on a chain, slow proverbs, "kanna". *Wants* her Thursday darshan and fine flour. The secret riddle-solver: your hints.
- **Rasheed-saab**, 40, Immu's Abba. Cyber Towers programmer; practises "gas station" in the mirror. *Wants* Immu to have more choices than he had.
- **Bunty-anna**, 19, Chinnu's cousin. RX100, Ameerpet Java course, denim in May. "Tension nakko, I'm here." *Wants* an "onsite" posting. Gives races.
- **Faramarz uncle**, 58 (81 in 2026). White cap and cardigan, at the *galla* of Café Noor-e-Iran (fictional) beside Charminar. "Do pauna, ek plate Osmania!" *Wants* nobody nursing one chai all day. Swears his cellar stair "goes to Golconda".
- **Havaldar Pochaiah**, 50, RTC X Roads traffic cop: whistle, Luna, soft heart. "Ruko! Three on one cycle? Utro!" *Wants* one quiet day.
- **Yadagiri-anna**, 45, auto driver, paid hint (₹5 drops you near the answer). "Meter nakko, das upar." *Wants* exact change.

## How it plays

- **City.**
  - *Moving:* run, rent or "borrow" cycles (₹2/hour), ride Bunty's Luna, Yadagiri's auto, red buses (fast travel from `koti_bus_stand` and `imlibun_bus_station`) or the Lumbini boat.
  - *Mission givers:* Immu, Amma, Ammamma, Bunty and Faramarz uncle.
  - *Base:* `irani_cafe_2` (Café Gulistan); its STD booth saves.
- **The tape.** Each mission ends ● REC: one city sound onto Side A, one token into the tin. The tape counter (000–999) is the progress bar.
- **Clues.** Immu's `cassette_*` riddles name landmarks visible on the map; hints: Ammamma, Yadagiri, the Journal.
- **Chappal Meter.**
  - 1: a colony uncle. 2: the Gurkha's whistle and the aunties' phone tree. 3: Pochaiah on foot. 4: Pochaiah's Luna. 5: MUMMY by auto, with a dodgeable homing chappal.
  - *Caught:* "PAKDA GAYA!", a scolding and ₹2 off pocket money.
  - *Cool down:* home before the street lights, chai in a cafe family room, or jasmine from any `flower_cart_*` (−2).
  - *Holy places:* it freezes in temples, mosques and churches; pursuers wait at the gate.
- **Hidden.** 40 golis, 24 dedication cassettes, 12 rooftop kites and Immu's chalk arrows to `durgam_cheruvu`, a `falaknuma_palace` view and the Gowliguda hangar.

## Key scenes

**Opening (2026).** From his 14th-floor HITEC City desk, Srikanth can see Cyber Towers. The courier: a cassette marked SIDE A / SIDE B in eleven-year-old capitals, and a map with twelve circles. At Amma's flat he finds the 2-in-1 in the loft; Amma, 61, reaches for her chappal by reflex when he raises dust. PLAY. "Bo kaata!" The VHS image rewinds ◀◀ to ▶ PLAY TUE 14 JAN 2003 07:10: he is eleven, on a terrace, under a sky full of kites.

**Midpoint (Golconda).** Three claps under the dome; three come back from Bala Hissar, impossibly clear. Then Immu's breathless voice: the tunnel is real, and on Chand Raat he'll hide in it. The hunt turns conspiracy, but the player is secretly collecting a goodbye: Abba on the toy train, *agle baras jaldi aana* at the lake.

**Climax (Chand Raat, Charminar).** Floodlit minarets, crescent cut-outs, bangles stacked like fire, haleem steam. Immu vanishes into Laad Bazaar; both mothers mobilise (the meter shows two chappals of different sizes). Only Chinnu has the clues. From Charminar's first gallery ("five minutes only," says the caretaker) he spots a yellow raincoat ducking behind Café Noor-e-Iran. Faramarz uncle nods at the cellar stair "that goes to Golconda". Among the biscuit tins sits Immu, eating Osmania. Chinnu claps three times; three claps answer.
> Chinnu: "There's no tunnel, Immu." Immu: "I know." Chinnu: "But Golconda heard us. America will also hear."
> Faramarz, taking the tin: "Kitte din rakhun?" Both: "Till we come back. Pakka."

Mummy arrives, chappal raised, and hugs him without putting it down.

**Ending (at Charminar).** On Eid night a blinking light climbs out of Begumpet over the minarets. Side A clicks off; Side B, recorded in 2026, begins: "Chinnu. I wore Side A thin in New Jersey. Abba wants to see Charminar once more. Same cafe, Saturday, four o'clock. Bring a clap." Aged 34, you cross the plaza; running drains you in four seconds (the knees). Faramarz uncle, 81, squints from the galla: "You two are late. Teis saal late." Down comes the tin: the kite corner, token 47, the laughing photo. A clap from the doorway; Chinnu claps back without turning. Immu, an "America uncle" now, offers M&Ms; Abba orders "Do chai, ek plate Osmania." Pigeons lift around the minarets. ■ STOP. ## Glossary

*Amma / Nanna*, *Ammi / Abba*: Mom / Dad (Telugu, Urdu); *Ammamma*, *Nani*: grandma; *Thatha*: grandpa; *anna*: elder brother; *arre*: hey; *atta*: wheat flour; *baigan*: nonsense; *beta*: child; *haleem*: Ramzan wheat-and-meat stew; *bo kaata*: "cut!"; *bonam*: offering pot; *Chand Raat*: Eid's eve; *chappal*: slipper; *chashma wala*: the one with glasses; *darshan*: temple visit; *das upar*: ₹10 extra; *do / ek*: two / one; *galla*: cash till; *Ganpati Bappa*: dear Lord Ganesh; *girni*: flour mill; *gulal*: festival colour; *hallu*: gently; *intiki raa*: come home; *kanna*: dear child; *khaata*: credit book; *kitte din rakhun?*: how long do I keep it?; *kosaru*: a vendor's free extra; *lootna*: chase a cut kite; *moora*: a forearm's length; *nakko*: no, don't; *Numaish*: the Nampally fair; *nemmadiga*: slowly; *pakka*: sure; *pandal*: festival marquee; *pauna*: ¾ cup of Irani chai; *pech*: kite fight; *phokat*: free; *Pothuraju*: Bonalu guardian dancer; *repu*: tomorrow; *ruko*: stop; *surang*: tunnel; *teekha / meetha*: spicy / sweet; *teenmaar*: drumbeat; *teis saal*: twenty-three years; *utro*: get off; *agle baras jaldi aana*: come back soon next year.
