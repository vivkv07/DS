An ontology is data as graphs
Every fact is a triple: subject → predicate → object. Triples compose into a knowledge graph.
:Alice a :Customer
:Order_123 a :Order
:Order_123 :placedBy :Alice
:Order_123 :hasStatus :Shipped
:Refund_9 a :Refund
:Refund_9 :refunds :Order_123
:Refund_9 :paidTo :Alice
:Bob a :SupportRep
ONTOLOGY · classes, properties, rules
DATA · instances as triples
The schema (classes, properties, constraints) is the ontology. The instances are the data. Standards: RDF · RDFS · OWL · SPARQL.
