RDFS: domain and range give you inference for free
Declare what a property connects. Assert one fact. The reasoner fills in the rest.
DECLARE:teaches rdfs:domain :Teacher
DECLARE:teaches rdfs:range :Student
ASSERT (one fact):Bob :teaches :Scooter
INFERRED (zero extra code):Bob a :Teacher
:Scooter a :Student
reasoner running…
One asserted fact. Two inferred facts. Nobody had to write them.
Assert something that contradicts them and the reasoner notices. That is the beginning of a guardrail.
Why Agentic Systems Need Ontologies · Frank Coyle, UC Berkeley
