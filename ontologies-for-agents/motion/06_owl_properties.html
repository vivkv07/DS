OWL: the constraints that catch real business errors
A few property declarations turn a graph into a rule-checker.

Transitive

:ancestorOf a owl:TransitiveProperty
CHAINS AUTOMATICALLYIf A→B and B→C then A→C. Org charts, part-of hierarchies, delegation chains.

Functional

:hasFather a owl:FunctionalProperty
AT MOST ONE VALUETwo "fathers" means they are the same person, or the data is wrong. Free duplicate detection.

Disjoint classes

:Customer owl:disjointWith :SupportRep
VIOLATIONBob is a SupportRep. A payee must be a Customer. Bob cannot be both, so the payout is rejected.
